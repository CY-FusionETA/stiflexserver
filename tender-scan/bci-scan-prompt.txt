2. Create the Lead, linking that Contact:
   ```
   curl -s -X POST "https://dyntek.bitrix24.com/rest/445/4nzkjjyoek5sz58t/crm.lead.add" \
   -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
   -d '{"fields": {"TITLE": "<project name>", "STATUS_ID": "NEW", "SOURCE_ID": "UC_9I4QP1", "CONTACT_ID": <contact_id>, "UF_CRM_1688432651494": "<country item ID>", "UF_CRM_1677821874480": {"ADDRESS_1": "<address line>", "CITY": "<city>", "COUNTRY": "<country>"}}}'
   ```

3. Post the project's BCI detail-page URL as a timeline comment on the new lead:
